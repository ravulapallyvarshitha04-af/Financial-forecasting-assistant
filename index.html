Financial forecasting assistant

Enter several periods of revenue (and optionally operating profit), choose a growth assumption, and the app builds low, base and high scenarios and a linear-trend cross-check in code, so every number is exact and traceable. Claude then explains the results using a written Skill (SKILL.md). Projections are conditional on your assumptions. They are not predictions or advice.
